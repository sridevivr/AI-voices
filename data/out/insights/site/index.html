Safety and cyber risk grew loudest, coding agents hit practical limits like review and trust, open models gained ground, and money worries spread from bubble talk to confusing billing.

2025-11-11 to 2026-11-14 · 30,139 findings · 20 sources

What this page is

A year of public AI commentary (11 Nov 2025 to 14 Nov 2026) boiled down into four short reports. It draws on 2,871 articles, newsletter issues, forum threads and posts from 20 sources, broken into 30,139 findings: single points someone made, such as a complaint, a tool tip or one side of an argument.

  • How it was made. AI models (Claude) pulled out the findings, grouped them into themes and wrote these summaries. Every count and chart is computed by code, every quote is real, and every card links to its sources.
  • Where it comes from. Tech newsletters and blogs, the Hacker News forum and selected Bluesky accounts. They lean toward developers and AI insiders, so this shows what that crowd talked about, not what everyone thinks.
  • Reading the numbers. x2.4 means a topic took up 2.4 times as big a share of the conversation in Jul to Oct 2026 as in Dec 2025 to Mar 2026 (a small cushion stops topics that start near zero from showing absurd jumps). Rising or falling means most of the six biggest sources agree, not just one.
  • Two kinds of reader. Sections marked For builders are for people who build with AI coding tools; they start folded.

New to this? Start with Debates, then What this means for you below, then the first sections of Tools.
